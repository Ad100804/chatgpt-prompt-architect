Use this after user approval. Delete irrelevant categories; do not fabricate unavailable resources or capabilities.

```text

## 1. Background and Context
[What exists today? Why does this task matter?]

## 2. Objective and Scope
[Target outcome, explicit boundaries, intended audience]

## 3. Sources
[S01] [title/url/rubric] — [purpose] — [verified / unverified / missing]

## 4. Resources
[R01] [file name/path/attachment] — [purpose] — [available / unverified / missing]

## 5. Skills and Plugins
[K01] [actual Skill] — [available? why needed?]
[P01] [actual Plugin/MCP] — [connected? optional? native alternative?]

## 6. Selected Approach
[O02] [name and implications]

## 7. Detailed Requirements
1. [Action, citing S01/R01 if needed]
2. [Action]

## 8. Constraints
[C01] [Non-negotiable rule]

## 9. Deliverables
[D01] [file/result and format]

## 10. Acceptance Criteria and Verification
- [Testable condition]
- [Verification step]

## 11. Execution and Approval Instructions
- Confirm access to required resources before acting.
- If essential inputs are missing, request them rather than guessing.
- Follow normal tool permissions and require confirmation for sensitive changes.
- Execute the approved task and report outcomes or blockers.
```
